# Enterprise IT Operations Guide

## Laptop Replacement Policy

Company laptops may be considered for replacement based on their age, physical condition, performance and ability to meet business requirements.

- Laptops that are more than four years old may be considered for replacement.
- Devices with repeated hardware failures, severe physical damage or performance issues that affect daily work may qualify for replacement.
- Employees must raise an IT support ticket requesting a laptop replacement.
- The request should include the asset tag, reason for replacement and a description of the device issues.
- Endpoint Support reviews the device condition and replacement eligibility.
- Final replacement approval must be obtained from the IT asset management team.
- A replacement device is issued only after the request has been reviewed and approved.

## Incident Priority Guide

IT incidents are classified into High, Medium and Low priorities based on their business impact and urgency.

### High Priority

High priority incidents cause major business disruption or prevent employees from performing critical work.

Examples:

- A critical business application is unavailable for multiple employees.
- An employee cannot access essential systems required for business operations.
- A major network outage affects an entire office.

High priority incidents must be reported immediately and escalated to the responsible support team.

### Medium Priority

Medium priority incidents affect an employee or a limited group of employees but do not completely stop overall business operations.

Examples:

- VPN connectivity problems affecting one employee.
- A laptop experiencing frequent performance issues.
- Email access problems affecting a small group of employees.

Employees should raise a support ticket with a clear description of the issue.

### Low Priority

Low priority incidents involve minor issues that have limited business impact and do not prevent normal work.

Examples:

- Printer configuration requests.
- Minor software usability questions.
- Requests for non-urgent software assistance.

These incidents can be handled through the normal IT support process.

## VPN Troubleshooting

Employees experiencing VPN connectivity issues should perform the following basic checks:

1. Confirm that the internet connection is working.
2. Verify that the correct VPN application is installed.
3. Check that the employee is using the correct login credentials.
4. Restart the VPN application and attempt to reconnect.
5. Restart the computer if the connection problem continues.
6. Record any error message displayed by the VPN application.

If the issue remains unresolved after these checks, employees should raise an IT support ticket.

Network Support is responsible for investigating VPN connectivity issues, authentication-related connection failures and network access problems.

Employees should include the error message, approximate time of failure and troubleshooting steps already attempted when raising a ticket.

## Lost or Stolen Device Procedure

Employees must immediately report any company laptop, mobile device or other IT equipment that has been lost or stolen.

The following procedure must be followed:

1. Inform the IT Service Desk immediately after discovering the loss or theft.
2. Provide the asset tag, device description, employee details and last known location if available.
3. Report the incident to the employee's reporting manager.
4. The IT Service Desk must escalate the incident to Endpoint Support and the IT Security team.
5. The IT team will assess the incident and take appropriate security measures, including account protection and device access restriction where applicable.
6. Employees must cooperate with any additional investigation or reporting requirements.

Lost or stolen devices must be treated as security-related incidents and escalated immediately.

## Software Installation Rules

Employees may install only software that has been approved by the company's IT department.

- Software must be obtained from approved and trusted sources.
- Employees must not install unauthorized, pirated or unlicensed software.
- Software installation requests must be submitted through the IT support ticketing process.
- The request must include the software name, business purpose and required access or license details.
- Endpoint Support reviews installation requirements and compatibility.
- Software requiring additional licenses, elevated privileges or security review must receive the appropriate IT approval before installation.
- Employees must not bypass security controls or attempt unauthorized software installations.

Software installation is permitted only after the required review and approval have been completed.

## Ticket Escalation Process

IT support tickets should be monitored and escalated when an issue cannot be resolved by the initially assigned support team.

The escalation process is as follows:

1. The assigned support team reviews the ticket and performs initial troubleshooting.
2. If the issue remains unresolved, the support team records the troubleshooting steps and findings in the ticket.
3. The ticket is escalated to the appropriate specialist support team when additional technical expertise is required.
4. High priority incidents involving major business disruption must be escalated immediately.
5. If a ticket remains unresolved after escalation, the support team must notify the IT Service Desk lead or responsible IT manager.
6. The ticket must include its current status, impact, troubleshooting history and reason for escalation.

Employees may request an update from the IT Service Desk if their issue remains unresolved.

## Personal Internet Reimbursement

The supplied IT operations guide does not specify an amount or reimbursement policy for personal internet usage.

Employees should contact the HR or Finance department for clarification. The IT assistant must not invent or assume a reimbursement amount.
